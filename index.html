Questions

Fair questions.

01What is EverythingZaroori?

A collection of small web apps for daily life. Each one handles a single everyday job: planning, money maths, shopping, and more as the suite grows.

02What does “zaroori” mean?

It's Hindi for “necessary” or “essential”. The idea is simple: everything you actually need, nothing you don't.

03Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser on phone, tablet, or laptop.

04Which apps are available?

The SIP calculator preview is live on this page. The calendar and curated stores are coming soon, with more after that.

05Is the SIP calculator financial advice?

No. It shows an estimate based on a fixed rate of return. Real returns go up and down, so treat it as a rough guide.

06Can I suggest an app?

Please do. Write to hello@everythingzaroori.com with the zaroori tool you wish existed.
